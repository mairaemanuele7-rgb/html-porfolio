The Best Movies According to Me

My top 3 movies of all-time

The Nightmare Before Christmas

A stop-motion film beautifully created by Tim Burton that talks about the holiday season and the importance of being true to yourself.

The Greatest Showman

A musical biographical film about P.T. Barnum and his circus empire, highlighting themes of ambition, perseverance, and the power of dreams.

Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves

A fantasy adventure film based on the popular tabletop RPG, following a group of adventurers on a quest to save their kingdom.
